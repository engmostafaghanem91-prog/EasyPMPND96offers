Smart Puzzles

Two interactive thinking and reasoning tests, 40 questions each, with pictures, numbers and words. Pick a grade to start.

Grade 1

40 questions, about 25 minutes, with a read-aloud button

  • Word groups
  • Sentence completion
  • Number puzzles
  • Number series
  • Figure matrices
  • Paper folding and shapes

Grade 4

40 questions, about 30 minutes

  • Verbal analogies
  • Word groups and sentences
  • Number puzzles
  • Number series
  • Figure matrices and groups
  • Paper folding and spatial

How it works

  • One question at a time, with three or four choices.
  • After you answer, the right choice turns green. Then press Next.
  • For Grade 1: read the question together, or press the speaker button.
  • Keyboard: press 1 to 4 to choose, and Enter for Next.

For parents and teachers

This is a fun practice test of thinking skills. It is not a standardized IQ test and does not give an official IQ score. For a formal assessment, see a licensed school psychologist.

The questions follow the three kinds of reasoning used in American school ability tests: verbal, quantitative and nonverbal (figures and paper folding). The math matches the Common Core standards for each grade.

At the end you see the score, the result for each skill, the strongest skill, the skill to practice, and a review of every missed question.
