Notes for the teacher

  • Read every question title aloud to the class before the students start (Grade 1 students are still early readers).
  • Do not read the story in Question 8 aloud — it measures the student's own reading.
  • Accept clear answers even if letter formation is not perfect; spelling of the target word in Q3 must be correct.
  • Suggested grading: 45–50 Excellent • 38–44 Very Good • 30–37 Good • 25–29 Pass • below 25 Needs support.
